Privacy Policy — Rent Ledger

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Rent Ledger (Kira Defteri, "the app") is developed by Cumhur Çetinkaya, an independent developer working as an individual under the studio name We Code Future ("we", "us").

Data controller

Cumhur Çetinkaya (individual developer, trading as We Code Future) · destek@wecodefuture.net

Summary

We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. Everything you enter in the app stays on your device.

Data we collect

None. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs that collect data.

Data stored on your device

Leases, rent amounts, payment records, receipt photos and scanned documents, manually entered rates and your settings are stored only on your device and are included in your device backups under Apple's terms. You can erase all of it at any time in Settings → "Delete all data". Deleting the app also removes it.

Rate updates

To keep official inflation rates current, the app occasionally downloads a public rates file from wecodefuture.net (hosted on GitHub Pages). This request contains no identifier and no data you entered. As with any website, the hosting provider may process your IP address to deliver the file; we do not receive or store it.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment information; the app only receives Apple's confirmation that a purchase is valid in order to unlock Rent Ledger Pro.

Permissions

The app asks for camera access only when you scan a receipt, and for notification permission only when you turn on reminders. To attach a photo it uses the system photo picker: it receives only the photo you choose and cannot see your other photos. Photos and documents stay on your device. You can change permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

Sharing

When you share a rent-increase message, the text goes only to the app or person you choose through the iOS share sheet.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Your rights (GDPR / KVKK)

To exercise your rights, write to destek@wecodefuture.net. Because we do not collect personal data, we hold no data about you to access, correct or delete. If you email us, we use your address only to reply and delete the conversation on request. If you use Send Feedback, the draft email includes the app version, iOS version, device model and language so we can help; you can edit or remove them before sending.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date.

Contact

destek@wecodefuture.net · wecodefuture.net
