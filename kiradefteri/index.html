Rent Ledger

Coming soon to the App Store · iPhone

Calculate the legal rent increase in Türkiye and keep your leases and payments in one place.

  • Statutory cap from the 12-month average CPI (TBK Art. 344), including historical rules.
  • Landlord and tenant modes; a ready-to-send increase message.
  • Payment ledger, receipt archive and renewal reminders.
  • No account, no ads; your data stays on your device.
